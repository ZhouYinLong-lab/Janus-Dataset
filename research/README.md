# Research files

This directory contains the working materials for Stage 2 of Janus-Dataset: literature landscape mapping and structured comparison.

## Files

- [`research_protocol.md`](research_protocol.md): scope, definitions, inclusion criteria, and evidence levels.
- [`search_queries.md`](search_queries.md): query families and database-specific search notes.
- [`search_log.csv`](search_log.csv): record of the first-pass searches.
- [`literature_matrix.csv`](literature_matrix.csv): seed-paper comparison table.
- [`research_tracker.md`](research_tracker.md): master tracking table combining completed research, new evidence, relevance ratings, and the next-work queue.
- [`evidence_ledger.md`](evidence_ledger.md): claims, supporting sources, and caveats.
- [`landscape_report.md`](landscape_report.md): current synthesis, novelty risks, and recommended next decisions.
- [`preliminary_assessment.md`](preliminary_assessment.md): polished current answer on prior work, achievements, and the remaining gap.
- [`briefing/`](briefing/): current Chinese scoping review, short briefing notes, claim-evidence matrix, and the 2026 novelty-check update.
- [`value_assessment/`](value_assessment/): four independent evidence chains covering problem existence, recoverability, model utility, and cost-benefit, with source tables and locally retained open papers.
- [`2026-09-30-negative-value-expanded-audit.md`](2026-09-30-negative-value-expanded-audit.md): expanded audit of direct molecular-model comparisons involving measured inactives, presumed negatives, untested-as-negative labels, and InertDB.
- [`2026-09-30-enzyme-negative-label-prior-art-comparison.md`](2026-09-30-enzyme-negative-label-prior-art-comparison.md): compares manual recovery, dense enzyme screens, inhibitor-supervision ablation, and computational decoy benchmarks; narrows the remaining enzyme-negative research question.
- [`2026-09-30-sabio-rk-cross-database-audit.md`](2026-09-30-sabio-rk-cross-database-audit.md): live cross-resource audit of SABIO-RK against the GH1 NIMS pilot; distinguishes compound/EC-level positive-kinetic matches from exact observation coverage and negative evidence.
- [`2026-09-30-theisen-patent-censoring-source-followup.md`](2026-09-30-theisen-patent-censoring-source-followup.md): traces three DOI-missing censored kinase rows to patent sources; classifies them as high-potency actives, not negative observations.
