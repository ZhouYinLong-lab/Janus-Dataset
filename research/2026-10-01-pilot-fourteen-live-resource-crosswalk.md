# Live resource crosswalk for the 14 enzyme-negative pilot observations

## Scope

The source-linked pilot currently contains 14 candidate table/prose observations from three papers (J043: NP001–NP004; J044: NP005–NP008; J037: NP009–NP014), plus the separate class-level statement NP015. This audit checks the three nearest broad chemical-bioactivity resources—ChEMBL, PubChem BioAssay, and ECBD—at source and exposed metadata level, and interprets lexical hits against the observation-level evidence. It supplements, but does not replace, the existing row-level BRENDA/SABIO-RK adjudications in [`database_coverage_adjudication_v0.csv`](pilots/enzyme-negative-literature/database_coverage_adjudication_v0.csv).

### Query-path positive controls

To check that zero results did not simply reflect a broken endpoint, the same interfaces were tested against known records: ChEMBL returned one document (CHEMBL1833806) for both DOI 10.1016/j.bmc.2011.05.033 and its known PMID 21669536; PubChem returned 27 AIDs for that known DOI and AID 620763 for `620763[AID]`. For target/accession fields, the official PUG REST target lookup for AID 1000 returned GI 116516899 and protein name “mevalonate kinase”; Entrez returned 8 results for `"mevalonate kinase"[PTN]`, 3 for `116516899[PTGI]`, and 3 (AIDs 1000, 557, 555) for versionless `YP_815859[GBAC]`. The versioned `YP_815859.1[GBAC]` query returned zero, so PubChem's index is demonstrably version-sensitive. Separately, documented PUG REST protein accession lookup returned an annotated protein summary and zero assay AIDs for pilot accession Q25BW5; the same accession/AID operations for control YP_815859 returned three AIDs (555, 557, 1000). ECBD metadata search recovered its known *A. fumigatus* assay title (three API objects, reflecting versions/records). These controls verify basic endpoint behavior, not sensitivity to every synonym, structure representation, linked reference, or unpublished record. PubChem's Entrez `pcassay` field list has no PMID field, so its PMID values should not be treated as an equivalent query route.

## PubChem lexical near-matches adjudicated

The PubChem Entrez query `"cellobiose"[All Fields] AND "hydrolysis"[All Fields]` returned nine AIDs: 620763 and 620754–620761. Their live PUG-REST descriptions link them to PMID 21669536 / DOI 10.1016/j.bmc.2011.05.033, a different study. The assay titles concern inhibition or activity of intestinal glycosidases using maltose and glucose readouts; none is one of the three Janus pilot sources or a cellobiose-negative observation from them. Thus a broad keyword hit is not an observation match. Likewise, `"arbutin"[All Fields] AND "beta-glucosidase"[All Fields]` returned AID 2101, a glucocerebrosidase inhibitor/activator screen, not LpBgl5 hydrolysis of arbutin.

This check is deliberately conservative: the near-matches demonstrate why substrate/assay vocabulary must be coupled to the measured entity, exact source, outcome, and conditions. They do not count as coverage or as new Janus records.

## Mapping back to the candidate observations

- **J043 / NP001–NP004 (LpBgl5):** exact DOI/protein/accession strings were not found in the scoped ChEMBL, PubChem, or ECBD lookups. BRENDA previously returned a different *L. plantarum* homolog/EC-related entry; that is a near-match, not coverage. SABIO-RK was not queried for this exact accession in the prior audit. Status: source-confirmed observations; exact broad-resource overlap **not established**, but not proven globally absent.
- **J044 / NP005–NP008 and NP015 (BglM-G1/H75R):** exact source/protein terms were not found in the scoped broad-resource queries. BRENDA/SABIO-RK previously showed only L-configured substrate analogs in the scoped enzyme-resource checks; the two H75R dashes remain source-outcome ambiguous regardless of database search. Status: NP005/NP007 are source-supported qualitative no-detect candidates; NP006/NP008 remain unresolved; no global absence claim.
- **J037 / NP009–NP014 (Q25BW5–cellobiose):** PubChem protein accession lookup returns the exact Q25BW5 protein summary (beta-glucosidase 1A, *P. chrysosporium*) but no associated assay AIDs; broader PubChem lexical cellobiose hits trace to different sources and maltose-inhibition assays. Existing SABIO-RK records match the same protein/substrate pair under a different 2008 source and 30 °C regime; BRENDA has a separate-source qualitative comment. Neither covers the six Heins 2014 `<0.1` condition-specific measurements. Status: exact protein entity is represented in PubChem, but no linked BioAssay AID or exact source/assay/condition observation coverage was established through the queried pathways.

## What changed in the mainline assessment

The 14-row pilot now has more than enzyme-resource searches: there are bounded live source/metadata checks against ChEMBL, PubChem BioAssay, and the exposed ECBD assay/target index. These checks support only “no exact match established in these scoped queries.” They do **not** estimate field-wide undercoverage, publication-bias prevalence, or extraction yield, because the pilot was purposively assembled from three known papers and the databases were not exhaustively matched at every chemical/assay row. The next valid step is a larger, pre-specified paper/assay sample with a logged query strategy, followed by blinded manual adjudication of exact versus near matches.
